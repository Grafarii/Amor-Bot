## Title
Offline Avoidance: A Behavioral Construct Proposal on Escape From Physical-World Contingencies Through Prolonged Immersive Social Virtual Reality Use

## Description
Theoretical article proposing offline avoidance as a behavioral construct: immersive social VR used to escape, postpone, or reduce contact with aversive offline contingencies (social demands, bodily self-presentation, tasks, and transition distress). Complements virtual dysphoria and virtual confabulation. Author: André Cicivizzo, UNIP.
